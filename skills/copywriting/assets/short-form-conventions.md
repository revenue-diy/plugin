# SHORT-FORM CONVENTIONS

> **Version 0.1** | 2026-10-15

Posts, social updates and captions, and the headlines and hooks that open any piece: copy that lives or dies on its first line. The client's channel rules in the context win wherever they differ from this file.

---

## THE SHAPE

Hook → re-hook → body → close → call to action. One idea from top to bottom; a second idea is the next post.

---

## THE HOOK

Most of the effort goes here: a weak first line means the rest is never read.

- **Short enough to take in at a glance on a phone**, before any "see more" cut.
- **A statement that delivers**, not a tease of one: the claim, the number or the named thing itself.
- **Offer 2 to 4 options, each a different type**, and let the user pick:

| Type | Shape |
|---|---|
| **Moment in time** | "Last Tuesday, a client…" |
| **Contrarian take** | a position most of the audience holds, flatly disputed |
| **Admission** | a mistake or a fear, owned |
| **Surprising fact** | a sourced number or finding that breaks an assumption |
| **Declarative** | a short, blunt truth aimed at the reader |
| **Result** | a specific outcome, with its number |

---

## THE BODY

- **Re-hook** – one line after the hook that keeps the momentum and sets up the point.
- **Mobile first** – short sentences, single-sentence paragraphs where they add punch, white space between beats.
- **Proof woven in** – a number, a named example or the writer's own experience, inside the body rather than parked at the end.
- **One pattern break** – a turn the reader did not expect: a contrarian point, a surprising number, an honest admission.

---

## CLOSE AND CALL TO ACTION

- **The close** – one sentence that lands the idea of the hook.
- **One call to action, matched to the goal:**
  - conversation – a genuine question the reader can answer from their own experience;
  - conversion – the direct next step, named;
  - resource – an offer to send or share something specific.
- Never a vague "Thoughts?" and never two asks.

---

## FORMAT AND LIMITS

- **The channel's limit** – from the client's channel rules where the context holds them, else the platform's own current limit; check it, never guess it.
- **Line breaks** as the reader will see them on the platform.
- **Emojis, symbols and hashtags** only as the brand allows and only where each earns its place.

---

## DELIVERY EXTRAS

- The character count against the limit.
- The hook type used, and the unused hook options for testing.

---

## COMMON FAILURES

- Two ideas in one post.
- A hook that teases instead of delivering.
- Generic authority ("many leaders say…") in place of a name or a number.
- A wall of text with no white space.
- A close that summarises instead of landing.
