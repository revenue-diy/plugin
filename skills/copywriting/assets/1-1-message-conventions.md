# ONE-TO-ONE MESSAGE CONVENTIONS

> **Version 0.1** | 2026-10-15

Emails and direct messages to one person or a small named group: a first touch, a follow-up, a reply, a meeting request, an introduction, a thank-you, a hard message. The client's outbound and email rules in the context win wherever they differ from this file.

---

## THE RULES

- **Written to one person** – by name, about their situation, in the register of the relationship.
- **The reason first** – the first line says why you are writing to them, and why now.
- **About them before about you.**
- **One ask** – specific, and easy to say yes to.
- **Short** – reads on a phone screen without scrolling, unless the content truly needs more.
- **Real personalisation only** – a specific detail from their world (something they wrote, a change at their company, a result), taken from the material. Never a detail made up about the person, and never a mail-merge compliment.
- **Plain and human** – no marketing voice in a personal message.

---

## THE SHAPE

| Part | Job |
|---|---|
| **Subject line** (new email thread) | short and specific, written like a colleague's, never clickbait; a reply keeps the thread's subject |
| **Opening** | the reason for writing – a trigger, a shared context, or their own words |
| **The point** | what is in it for them: the insight, the offer, the answer |
| **The ask** | one, concrete and low-effort: a yes-or-no question, two times, a link |
| **Sign-off** | in the sender's own voice |

---

## BY TYPE

| Type | What it needs |
|---|---|
| **First touch** | the trigger, why it matters to them, one low-effort ask – no attachment, no pitch dump |
| **Follow-up** | something new each time (a resource, an insight, a question) and a one-line pointer to the last message – never "just bumping this" |
| **Reply** | their question answered first, then anything else |
| **Meeting request** | the purpose, the length, two concrete times or a booking link |
| **Introduction** | why these two people, what each gets, then step out of the way |
| **Hard message** | the news in the first line, the reason in one more, what happens next – no padding |
| **Thank-you or recap** | the one thing that mattered, then the agreed next steps with owners and dates |

---

## TONE

Match the relationship – a stranger, a prospect, a client, a colleague – and mirror the register of their last message. Formal only where they are.

---

## DELIVERY EXTRAS

- Two or more subject-line options for a new thread.
- Whether a follow-up is worth scheduling, and when – the user's call.

---

## COMMON FAILURES

- Opening on "I hope this finds you well" or "My name is…".
- More than one ask.
- Personalisation that could fit anyone.
- A pitch dressed up as a question.
- Urgency that is not real.
