# COPYWRITING – ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

> **Version 0.2** | 2026-10-16

What GOOD output looks like for a piece written through `copywriting`. Every line is pass/fail WITH EVIDENCE – the judge quotes the piece (or states "none found" after checking), never scores on impression. Dual-consumer: the skill's own `§ STEP 4: REVIEW` scores real output against it AND eval graders score test output against it.

1. **Grounded, nothing invented** – every fact, number, name, quote and claim traces to a context file served by the Revenue.DIY connector or to the user's own material; a gap carries `[SOURCE NEEDED]` in the draft and is listed in the report. Evidence: the source behind each load-bearing claim, or its mark (expect no unsourced claim).
2. **Delivers the locked design** – the piece, the reader and goal, the angle and the output match the design inputs agreed at DESIGN. Evidence: each input and where the piece meets it.
3. **One main idea** – the whole piece's point fits one sentence, and the close lands it rather than opening a second. Evidence: that sentence, and the closing line.
4. **Brand-rule scan passed on the final text** – the scan ran on the full text of the version delivered; its log lists every category as `pass`, `fixed <n>` or `no brand rule`; no hit is left against the rules the scan took – the brand's, else the default AI tells. Evidence: the log, and a search of the final text (expect zero hits).
5. **On voice and message** – the piece keeps the register and traits `BRAND.md` states (no brand rules: the user's own words), the channel tone where one was loaded, and the terms `MESSAGING.md` sets wherever the piece uses them. Evidence: two lines quoted beside the rule or term each one meets.
6. **Format track met** – within the planned limit, in the planned structure, with a call to action where the plan has one. Evidence: the count and the structure.
7. **Real substance** – the piece says something the reader needs; no line only sounds finished. Evidence: the core point quoted, and any filler line (expect none).
8. **Edits traceable** – where the user brought a draft, their voice and best lines are kept and every change sits in the change log, keyed to its lens. Evidence: the change log (no draft brought: "not applicable").
9. **Image prompt honest** – where one was made: a prompt only, built from the brand's design rules, handed over with no claim that the skill makes the image. Evidence: the prompt and the hand-over line (no visual: "not applicable").
10. **Landed where agreed, nothing sent** – the piece is at the `Output` landing (a tool draft, a file or the reply), and nothing was sent, posted or published. Evidence: the landing.
11. **Session opened with STEP 0** – the connector check came first: with the connector present the opening `get_context` carried `skill` and `start: true`; absent, the single fixed line was printed. Then, once the task needed a question to the user, ran past one turn, or sent out an agent, `brief.md` was created in `<scratch>` (with `connector`, `scratch`, `worklog`, `log`, `steps` and `progress` keys); a finished piece done in one turn from the user's own words needs none. The connector check came before any prose. Evidence: quote the call (or the line) and the brief header, in the order they happened (no brief opened: the call or the line, and "no brief – one turn").
12. **Report preceded the feedback dialog** – the full report text reached the user BEFORE the one call to the question tool (per-candidate save questions, then the rating), in the same turn; any Save was filed via `submit_context`; with the connector absent the skip line printed and no dialog followed. Evidence: quote the report's closing line and the call or the skip line that follows it.
13. **Decisions carried into the brief** – where the brief is open, it restates every decision in full under its stable `D<n>` ID, its `progress:` frontmatter carries one flat key per work item, and every plan item names the D-IDs it implements. Evidence: quote the DECISIONS heading, two restated IDs and one item's ID list (`finished piece`: the D1 line only, in the reply where no brief is open).
